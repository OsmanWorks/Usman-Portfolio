Data Sources & Limitations

  • Open-Meteo — Forecast & air quality. Free, keyless, updated hourly.
  • RainViewer — Live radar mosaic, past 2 hours, 10-min steps.
  • NASA GIBS — Satellite true-colour imagery, most recent available daily pass.
  • CARTO / OpenStreetMap — Base map tiles.

Known limitations of this prototype

  • PMD official radar feeds and NDMA advisories are not yet integrated — this needs a formal data-sharing arrangement.
  • Lightning strikes, storm-cell tracking and animated wind-particle layers are phase-2 items.
  • Push notifications, offline PWA caching and a native app all need a real backend and hosting that a single front-end file can't provide.
